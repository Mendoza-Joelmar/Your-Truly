Why I Chose This Site

Okay, so like—I’m kind of a huge nerd for anything techy. Editing stuff, making videos, figuring out how to make things look ✨cool✨—it’s my jam. Plus, I get to learn about others through this. What they want, what they like, anything.

I started this site mostly for fun, but also because I wanted a place to throw all my experiments, brain dumps, and passion projects into the void.

I’m still learning (always learning, tbh), but that’s what makes it exciting. Every time I figure out a new technique in whatever I'm doing, like learning a new shortcut or how to better edit a video? Dopamine. Pure digital euphoria

This place is my little playground. You’re welcome to poke around and see what I've brewed. Enjoy the mess~~
